Spots by area

WHERE TO GO
Chinatown
FOOD & SHOPPING
Yokohama Chinatown

Japan's largest Chinatown — good for street food and browsing shops, however long you have.

Sakuragicho
AMUSEMENT
Yokohama Cosmo World

An amusement park with a Ferris wheel and rides. Entry is free — you pay per ride.

Cup Noodles Museum

A hands-on museum where you design your own cup noodles. Right next to Cosmo World.

These two are within walking distance of each other — a good pair for a single day.

Minato Mirai
VIEWS & SHOPPING
Osanbashi Pier

The rooftop deck of the passenger terminal, with sweeping views of the Minato Mirai skyline. Especially good after dark.

Yamashita Park

An easy waterfront stroll, with the historic Hikawa Maru ship as a backdrop.

Yokohama Red Brick Warehouse

A former harbor warehouse now filled with shops, cafés, and restaurants.

Motomachi
SHOPPING
Motomachi Shopping Street

A short walk from Chinatown, with a quieter mix of boutiques and cafés.

A quieter, more traditional side of Japan
JAPANESE SCENERY
Sankeien Garden

A traditional Japanese garden with historic buildings, and a calm alternative to the busier spots in town.

Yokohama Park

A park next to Yokohama Stadium, pleasant for seasonal flowers.

Good with kids
FAMILY-FRIENDLY
Yokohama Anpanman Children's Museum

A popular stop for younger children. The shop area is open without a museum ticket, too.

Yokohama Cosmo World and the Cup Noodles Museum (see Sakuragicho, above) are also great with kids.
